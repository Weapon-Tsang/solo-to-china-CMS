# SoloToChina Content Production Strategy 1.1

## Overview

SoloToChina turns human-selected China-travel sources into original, evidence-bounded English content for independent international visitors. A saved source is never automatically treated as an article. The pipeline is entity-first, fact-first, structured-first, and always keeps the final publishing decision with a human.

Strategy 1.1 succeeds Strategy 1.0 without rewriting it. The active version and its concise evolution history are defined in `config/content-strategy.json`; prior specifications remain immutable records of the rules that applied when earlier records were created.

## Core principles

- Human discovery and explicit capture only; never crawler behaviour.
- Claims and Knowledge retain source provenance. Commercial data remains outside Research.
- AI recommends an action; it does not publish or silently create an article.
- Only an explicit **Approve article** decision can start content planning.
- Missing, stale, conflicting, or unverified facts are omitted or marked `NEEDS_RESEARCH` rather than invented.

## Strategy evolution and record continuity

- The dashboard exposes an ordered strategy evolution log with an effective date, a concise summary, and the material changes in each version.
- New intake, recommendation, opportunity, brief, draft, QA, WordPress, and visual records carry the active Strategy `1.1` tag.
- Existing records retain the version that created them. A strategy upgrade never backfills an older record as if it had followed newer rules.
- Any future change requires a new immutable specification, a new manifest entry, a succinct change summary, test coverage, handoff updates, and a passing release check.

## Intake intelligence and recommendation

Every AI-processed source receives a strategy-tagged intake analysis. It records primary topic, entities, knowledge points, information density, completeness, article potential, duplicate likelihood, missing information, and a concise user-facing rationale. It produces one of `ARTICLE_CANDIDATE`, `KNOWLEDGE_ONLY`, `CLAIM_ONLY`, `CLUSTER_CANDIDATE`, `RESEARCH_REQUIRED`, `DUPLICATE`, `LOW_VALUE`, or `UNSURE`.

The recommendation offers one of five human decisions: approve article, knowledge only, add to cluster, research first, or ignore. Knowledge/claim extraction may still enrich the evidence base; only approval opens the article-planning job.

## Content opportunities and canonical content

Opportunities aggregate evidence across Claims, Knowledge, entities, and topics. Their readiness records coverage rather than treating a single post as sufficient research.

An approved opportunity creates Canonical Travel Content: content type, destination, intent, audience, query set, answer-first summary, highlights, practical tips, warnings, FAQ, answer blocks, image plan, SEO package, schema package, and verification state. The planner turns this structured object into a fact-linked outline; it does not write HTML directly.

Supported content types are city guide, itinerary, attraction guide, food guide, transport guide, neighborhood guide, hotel-area guide, shopping guide, practical guide, first-time guide, comparison, listicle, and how-to.

## Image evidence and reader trust

An image captured after a user explicitly saves a source is retained as internal multimodal evidence. It can contribute to OCR, scene/context understanding, factual extraction, and the traceable research record. This use does not turn the source post into an English translation or automatically make its media a publication asset.

Real-world photography can materially improve reader trust when it is release-ready: it is owned by SoloToChina, supplied by an official source, licensed for the intended use, or permission has been confirmed and recorded. In that case, the photo may be selected for the article when it genuinely supports the surrounding claim and has accurate alt text and a caption where appropriate.

Contextual fit, a mention in the source, or a useful depiction alone is not a release authorization. A third-party source photo that has not cleared this release condition stays in the research layer and is never automatically copied into a public article. `REAL_WORLD_PHOTO` therefore requests a release-ready real image and is never fabricated by an image model.

`INFOGRAPHIC` and `MAP_OR_ROUTE` require deterministic structured renderers and validated data. Only `ILLUSTRATION` can be generated by the configured image provider, and it must not assert unverified facts. Image failure never discards an otherwise valid Draft.

## Article, SEO, GEO, and Schema.org

Articles begin with a direct answer, use descriptive H2/H3 structure, include reader-facing key takeaways and FAQs, and avoid generic travel copy. SEO is natural-language intent matching: one primary keyword, secondary questions, concise metadata, canonical URL only when configured, and no invented internal links.

GEO is not a special markup type. It uses clear questions, direct answers, consistent entities, structured facts, answer blocks, and scannable headings. Schema.org is deterministic from Canonical Content: WebPage, Article, BreadcrumbList, Organization, and ImageObject are emitted only when their underlying values exist. No coordinates, prices, booking rules, or other unknown values are fabricated.

## QA and publishing

QA checks strategy-version continuity, title/H1 contract, heading hierarchy, evidence and conflict safety, placeholder/HTML safety, SEO metadata, schema consistency, image strategy, and commercial isolation. Rendering uses structured content blocks and produces a WordPress payload tagged with the same strategy version. WordPress delivery remains draft-only.
