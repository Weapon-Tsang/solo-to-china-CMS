# The Feasible 3-Day Chongqing Route: Pacing, Taxis, and Vertical Navigation

Chongqing rewards slow, deliberate exploration. This three-day route follows the three illustrated day maps below: the layered Yuzhong riverfront on Day 1, the south-bank hillside and a short ferry experience on Day 2, then Eling, Liziba and the city's cultural and Jiangbei districts on Day 3. The arrows in the maps show a suggested order, not guaranteed point-to-point travel times. Hills, stairs, traffic, opening hours and river conditions can all change the plan.

## Before You Set Out: Read the City in Three Dimensions

A destination that appears close on a map may be separated by several road levels. Check the exact entrance and street level before committing to a staircase, and allow extra time around the riverfront. Use the metro for longer transfers; consider a taxi or ride-hailing car when a steep connection would make the day needlessly tiring. Check current fares and the final stop shown on the platform rather than relying on a fixed fare or time estimate in an old itinerary.

Wear shoes with good grip, keep water with you, and treat each day as a menu of stops. If the full circuit feels too long, skip a stop rather than rushing or crossing the city just to match a picture.

## Day 1: Mountain City Trail to the Riverfront

**Suggested order:** Mountain City Trail (山城步道) → Jiefangbei (解放碑) → Baixiangju (白象居) → Raffles City Chongqing / Chaotianmen (来福士 / 朝天门) → Hongyadong (洪崖洞) → Jiangtan Park riverfront (江滩公园).

Start on a manageable section of Mountain City Trail to see the older stairways and the city's stacked streets. The trail has multiple entrances; choose one that suits your energy level and confirm where it exits before walking on to Jiefangbei. At Jiefangbei, take a break in the pedestrian centre before heading toward Baixiangju. Baixiangju is a residential complex, so use public access areas only and keep noise down for residents.

Continue toward Raffles City and the Chaotianmen waterfront. This is a substantial urban transfer despite how compact it can look on a map; a car or a changed walking route may be more sensible than forcing the map's arrow. Save Hongyadong for later in the day if you want its illuminated facade. Finish at a suitable Jiangtan Park riverfront viewpoint, checking the precise park entrance and your return route before the light fades. The map's walking and taxi labels are illustrative estimates, not a promise that every segment will take that long.

## Day 2: South-Bank Heritage, Then Liangjiang Xiao Ferry

**Suggested order:** Huangjueya Old Street (黄桷垭老街) → Huangge Ancient Road (黄葛古道) → Longmenhao Old Street (龙门浩老街) → Xiahaoli (下浩里) → Clock Tower Square (钟楼广场) → Liangjiang Xiao Ferry (两江小渡).

Begin around Huangjueya Old Street and descend only the section of Huangge Ancient Road that matches your fitness and weather. The old road links the hillside to lower south-bank areas; **the full historic route is much longer than the map's “about 15 minutes” arrow**. Do not interpret that label as a through-hike time. If the path is wet, crowded or tiring, take road transport to the next stop instead.

Move on to Longmenhao Old Street and the nearby Xiahaoli lanes. These stops are best treated as a relaxed heritage-and-river-view interval rather than a timed sprint. Continue to Clock Tower Square if it fits your route, then make the ferry an optional finale. The supplied ferry photos show Liangjiang Xiao Ferry, but they do not establish today's boarding pier, sailing time or operating status. Check the operator's current route and ticket information on the day, and have a land-based return plan if the service is suspended or sold out.

## Day 3: Eling, Liziba, Civic Landmarks and Jiangbei

**Suggested order:** Eling Park (鹅岭公园) → Liziba (李子坝) → Chongqing People's Great Hall (人民大礼堂) → Three Gorges Museum (三峡博物馆) → Guanyinqiao (观音桥) → Beicang Cultural Park (北仓文创园).

Start in Eling Park for a gentler opening and city views. Continue to Liziba to observe the monorail passing through the building from a safe public viewing area. The change in elevation between these stops can make a short map distance slower than expected. The map suggests about 20 minutes on foot, but choose a car or transit if the climb or descent is not comfortable.

Travel to the People's Great Hall and Three Gorges Museum. They face the same civic square, so they form a natural pair; check museum opening hours before planning to go inside. If time is tight, enjoy the exterior and square without forcing a rushed museum visit. Later, cross to Jiangbei for Guanyinqiao and finish with the smaller streets and shops around Beicang Cultural Park. The map's metro and walking times are approximate and do not include waiting, station exits or detours.

## Pacing and Practical Decisions

This is a route framework, not an obligation to complete every stop. Keep the hillside walking on Day 2 flexible, allow extra time at major stations, and book no tight onward connection immediately after a ferry. To ask for less heat when ordering, show the phrase “微微辣” (very mildly spicy); spice levels still vary by kitchen. For current ferry operations, transit fares and venue hours, rely on the operator or venue on your travel day rather than on the figures printed in a saved image.
